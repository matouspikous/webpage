Privacy Policy for Wortballon

Last updated: September 2026

Wortballon is a German spelling and vocabulary practice application. This Privacy Policy outlines how user data is handled.

Data Collection

Wortballon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The application functions completely offline and does not connect to external servers, third-party analytics, or advertising services.

Local Storage

Game statistics, progress, and review preferences are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or third parties.

Third-Party Services

Wortballon integrates no third-party tracking, SDKs, or external advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

Because Wortballon collects no personal information, it strictly complies with privacy standards for users of all ages, including children.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support page.
