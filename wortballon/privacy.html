Privacy Policy for Wortballon

Last updated: September 2026

Wortballon is a German-language spelling learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what user data the app collects, processes, or shares.

Data Collection

Wortballon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app functions entirely offline and does not connect to any external server, analytics service, or third party.

Data Storage

Wortballon tracks your game progress, mistake history, and word review data locally on your device only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Third-Party Services

Wortballon does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

Since Wortballon collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, this page will be updated accordingly.
